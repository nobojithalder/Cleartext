A free AI text cleaner for copied drafts

        
Cleartext tidies text copied from AI writing assistants. Clean invisible Unicode, non-breaking spaces, long dashes, smart quotes, ellipsis characters, and leftover markdown before pasting text into a document, website, or code editor.

        

          

            
Clean formatting artifacts

            
Choose which characters and formatting marks to normalize. Review the before and after changes, then copy the cleaned text.

          

          

            
Review common writing patterns

            
Find Signals highlights selected phrases, repeated sentence openings, and formatting cues. These are editing prompts, not proof of who wrote the text.

          

          

            
Keep your text on your device

            
Text processing runs in your browser. Cleartext does not upload pasted text or require an account.
